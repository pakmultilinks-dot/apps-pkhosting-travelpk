How your data is protected

Retention7 daily copies
What's includedFiles + databases
Restore requestsFree via support ticket

Backups run automatically every night on all shared hosting plans at no extra cost. If you ever need a file or a full account restored, open a support ticket and our team will restore it for you free of charge.

We still recommend keeping your own off-site copy of critical data. If you need longer retention or custom backup schedules, ask about our VPS backup add-ons.
